How these are computed — Beta from 5 years of daily returns vs. SPY. 30-day volatility is annualised stdev of daily returns. Sharpe = (portfolio return − risk-free) ÷ total volatility; Sortino uses downside deviation only, so it doesn't penalise upside — generally the more useful of the two for a long-only book. Risk-free rate is the 3-month T-bill (DGS3MO) from FRED, free and keyless.

⚠️ Weighted volatility is Σ(wᵢ·σᵢ) and ignores correlation, so it overstates true portfolio risk — meaning these Sharpe and Sortino figures are conservative and your real ratios are likely better. The covariance-matrix fix is free (we already pull 5 years of history) and is the top v1.1 item.

⚠️ What this tab is — and what it is not

This applies Buffett's publicly stated, written criteria — durable moat, high returns on capital, low leverage, consistent cash generation, circle of competence, margin of safety — as a deterministic checklist. It is not a prediction of what Buffett would do, not affiliated with Berkshire, and not investment advice. No LLM is involved, which keeps it free, reproducible and auditable — and means it never invents a rationale. Thresholds are editable in config/buffett.yaml.
